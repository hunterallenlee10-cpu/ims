This Risk Disclosure explains some of the main risks of crypto investing and of using rules-based signals such as ours. It is not a complete list. If you do not understand these risks, do not act on any signal and consider speaking with a licensed professional.

## The short version

- Crypto is highly volatile.
- Past performance does not guarantee future results.
- You can lose some or all of your money.
- Do your own research and consider speaking with a licensed professional.

## Risk of loss

All investing carries risk. The value of crypto assets can fall sharply as well as rise, and you may lose some or all of the money you invest. Only invest money you can afford to lose.

## Crypto-specific risks

Crypto assets carry extreme price volatility, exchange and custody failure, loss of private keys, hacks and theft, limited regulation and investor protection, and fast-changing law and tax treatment. The signals cover crypto assets, including Bitcoin.

## Market volatility

Prices can move quickly and sharply, at any hour. A signal that is accurate when posted can be out of date minutes later.

## Leverage

Borrowing to invest, or using leveraged or derivative products, increases both potential gains and potential losses. Losses can exceed your original investment. Our signals are indicators, not recommendations to use leverage.

## Model and system risk

Our signals come from rules-based models. Models simplify reality and can fail. Rules that worked in the past can stop working, conditions can change in ways a model hasn't seen, inputs or data can contain errors or delays, and design choices can fit past data too closely ("overfitting").

## Hypothetical and backtested results

Any hypothetical or backtested result, if we ever show one, would be prepared with the benefit of hindsight, would not reflect real trading, fees, taxes, slippage or liquidity, and would have inherent limitations. No representation is made that any account will or is likely to achieve results similar to any hypothetical result.

## Timing and delivery

Signals are posted at a point in time and delivered through Telegram, a third-party platform that may be delayed or unavailable. Prices you can trade at may differ from prices when a signal was prepared.

## Liquidity, counterparty and platform risk

You may be unable to buy or sell when you want, at the price you want. Exchanges, wallets and custodians you use can fail, restrict withdrawals or suffer security breaches.

## Regulatory and tax risk

Laws, regulations and tax rules can change and can affect the value or legality of an investment. You are responsible for your own tax obligations.

Our signals are general and the same for every member of a tier. Deciding whether any investment is suitable for you, and in what amount, is your responsibility.
