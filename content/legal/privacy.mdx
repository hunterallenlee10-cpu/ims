## Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Investing Mastery Signals ("we", "us") collects and uses personal information. Contact: ceo@leeenterprisesunlimited.com.

## What we collect

- **Checkout:** your name, email address, billing country and the Telegram username you enter at checkout. Payments are handled by Stripe, which acts as the merchant of record; we do not see or store your full card details.
- **Membership:** your tier, subscription status and payment history, kept by Stripe.
- **Telegram:** your Telegram username and Telegram user ID, used to approve your join request and to manage your access to the groups.
- **Contact form:** your name, email address and message.
- **Usage data:** basic technical information such as pages visited and browser type, in our hosting provider's standard server logs. We do not currently use analytics or advertising tools.

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, exchange logins, wallet keys, passwords or other sensitive financial details.

## How we use it

- To provide your membership, send your invite link and manage your group access.
- To reply to messages you send us.
- To send service messages, such as billing notices, failed-payment notices and changes to our terms.
- To send research updates or marketing only where you have agreed or where the law allows, with an unsubscribe link in every message.
- To keep the Service secure and to understand how it is used.

## Legal bases

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on: performing our contract with you; our legitimate interests in running and improving the Service; your consent (for example, for marketing); and compliance with legal obligations.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Service, under contracts that limit their use of it, or when required by law. Current providers: Stripe (payments and billing), Telegram (member groups), Vercel (website hosting), Google (Gmail, for contact form emails), n8n (contact form delivery).

## Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies that are needed for the site and checkout to work and, where applicable, for analytics. This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout and billing pages under its privacy policy.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including legal, tax and accounting requirements.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to withdraw consent. California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA. To make a request, email ceo@leeenterprisesunlimited.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

The Service is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

## Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

## Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new "last updated" date and, if changes are significant, notify members.
