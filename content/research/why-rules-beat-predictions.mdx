## What a rule gives you

A rule is written down before the decision. That gives you three things a prediction can't:

- **Consistency.** The same inputs produce the same output, every time.
- **A record.** You can look back and see exactly why a state changed.
- **Calm.** When markets get loud, the rule doesn't change with them.

## What a rule does not give you

Rules can be wrong. A system that has worked in the past can stop working. A rule is a way to make decisions consistently, not a promise about outcomes.
